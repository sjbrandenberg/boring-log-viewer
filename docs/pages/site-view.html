Site view: cross-sections and 3D

Send all the borings of a site at once and get back one web page with a 3D model of the layers, suggested cross-sections, every boring log, and a map.

An automatic interpretation

The layers between borings, in both the cross-sections and the 3D model, are connected by a set of rules, not by a geologist. The figures say so. Use them to get a first picture of a site and to spot inconsistencies, and check them against your own judgement before relying on them. How cross-sections are drawn explains the rules.

Making a site page

The site page is made by the API, from the borings you send it. Every boring needs a latitude and longitude in its metadata to appear in the sections, 3D model and map; borings without them are still shown as logs.

With Python, put the borings in a list and send them to api/site:

import json, pathlib, requests

borings = [json.loads(p.read_text(encoding="utf-8")) for p in sorted(pathlib.Path("nantou").glob("*.json"))]

r = requests.post("https://uclageo.com/boring-log-viewer/api/site",
                  json={"name": "Nantou", "borings": borings})
r.raise_for_status()
pathlib.Path("nantou.html").write_text(r.text, encoding="utf-8")

Open nantou.html in a browser. It's a single self-contained file: you can keep it, email it or put it on a website. Only the map pictures are loaded from the internet.

From the command line, with the same JSON in site.json:

curl -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" --data-binary @site.json \
     "https://uclageo.com/boring-log-viewer/api/site" -o site.html

The body can be:

  • { "name": "Nantou", "borings": [ ...boring logs... ] };
  • just a list of boring logs, [ ... ]; or
  • an AGS4 file (Content-Type: text/plain) or DIGGS file (Content-Type: application/xml): every borehole with strata becomes a boring of the site.

Up to 200 borings and 10 MB. Each boring is checked as for a single log; an error names the boring, e.g. /borings/2/layers/0/bottom for the first layer of the third boring.

From NGL

The Next Generation Liquefaction database uses this to show its sites: its Plot button draws one boring, and the site view draws them all.

What's on the page

Three tabs, each with its own options in the bar above the drawing, and a map beside them.

3D model
The borings and the layers between them as a block. Drag to turn it, right-drag (or Shift-drag) to move it, scroll to zoom. Vertical exaggeration stretches it vertically; Opacity makes the layers see-through so you can see the borings and the water table inside. See The 3D model.
Cross-sections
The suggested sections (pick one under Section), with the borings at their distances along the line and the layers connected between them. Layers between borings switches between colours with labels and USCS hatches. See How cross-sections are drawn.
Boring logs
Each boring's log (pick one under Boring), the same as on the viewer page.
Map
The borings and the section lines. Click a boring to see its log, or a line to see that cross-section.

SVG and PNG download what's shown. For the 3D model, both save the current view (angle, exaggeration and opacity), with the legend; the SVG is vector throughout and opens in Illustrator or Inkscape. Units switches the sections and logs between metric and US units.

Suggested cross-sections

The sections are chosen from how the borings are laid out:

SectionLineDrawn when
AAlong the site: the direction the borings spread most, through their centre. It includes borings within 15% of its length of the line (at least 25 m), projected onto it.Always (with two or more located borings)
BAcross the site, at right angles to A, through the boring nearest the centreThe site is at least 20% as wide as it is long
Next letterThrough every boring: a bent line from boring to boring, in order along the site, so nothing is projectedA and B leave borings out, or project one from more than 10 m away
Next lettersAcross the site at 25% and 75% of the way alongMore than six borings, on a site at least twice as long as it is wide

A section that would show the same borings as an earlier one is left out. To draw a section along a line of your own, use api/site/section with line= (see the site API).

Getting the results as data

Add ?format=json to get the site's borings, suggested sections (with their SVG drawings) and the 3D model's layers as data instead of a page, for use in your own program. The parameters and the shape of the reply are in Render API: sites.
